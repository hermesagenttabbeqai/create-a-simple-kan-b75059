# User Guide

## What is this?

The Kanban Board is a simple tool to keep track of things you need to do. It has three columns — **TODO**, **IN PROGRESS**, and **READY** — and you move tasks between them as your work progresses. Everything is saved in your browser, so it is still there when you come back.

---

## Adding a task

1. Click **+ Add task** at the bottom of any column.
2. Type the task title in the box that appears.
3. Optionally type one or more tags, separated by commas (for example: `urgent, bug, feature`).
4. Click **Add**. The task appears in that column.

To close the form without saving, click **Cancel** or press Escape.

---

## Moving a task

**With the button:** Click the **→** button on a task card. The task moves to the next column (TODO → IN PROGRESS → READY → back to TODO).

**With drag and drop:** Click and hold a task card, drag it to another column, and drop it. This works on both mouse and touch screens.

---

## Editing a task

1. Click **Edit** on the task card.
2. Change the title or the tags.
3. Click **Save**.

---

## Deleting a task

Click **Delete** on the task card. The task is removed immediately.

---

## Tags

Tags are colored labels you add to a task to help organise your work. Use any words you like. A task can have any number of tags. Tags are shown as colored chips on the card.

---

## Your data

All tasks are saved in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Your board is private to your device. If you clear your browser storage, the tasks will be gone.

---
